Way 1: Compiler 📜

🔄📜

A compiler is the translator who wants the whole story first. It reads the whole program, then translates all of it at once.

First it translates everything — that's the implementation. Then the computer runs it — that's the execution. 🎉

Way 2: Interpreter ⚡

🔄⚡

An interpreter is the translator who works one line at a time. It translates one instruction, and the computer runs it right away.

Translating (implementation) and running (execution) happen together, step by step. ⚡

You Solved It All! 🎉🏆

A translator helps two languages understand each other.
A compiler translates everything, then runs it.
An interpreter translates and runs, one step at a time.
