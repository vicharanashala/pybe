Let's hear a story, before starting the concept.

Raghav is watching a movie in a very large theater.

Suddenly, there is a complete power failure, and the theater becomes completely dark. Nothing is visible.

After a few moments, an announcement is made: 

> **"Please remain seated. The emergency lights will turn on shortly."**

While waiting, Raghav becomes curious.

> **"Which row am I sitting in?"**

[Challenge] How can Raghav find out his row number?

Unfortunately, Raghav cannot see anything or leave his seat, so he cannot count just walk up and count the rows himself (since it has been announced to stay seated),  nor can he just look and count (since it is pitch dark).

- The only option Raghav has is to communicate with the person sitting directly in front of him.

[Ponder] If you were in Raghav's place, how would you find your row number?
Take few moments to come out with a plan, and then click on next to know how Raghav solved this problem.


Lets see, how Raghav found his row number.


Raghav asks the person (Tarun) sitting directly in front of him:

> **"What row are you sitting in?"**

The person (Tarun) in front does not know his row number either!
So he ask the person (Amrit) sitting directly in front of them the same question.

But the person (Amrit) sitting infront of Tarun also does not know his row number. So Amrit ask the person sitting infront of him the same question, and this continues.

This process continues until the question reaches the person sitting in the first row.

[Ponder] Before we see what happened next, lets check if this Raghav will work or not?

[Ponder Section : Start]
Will this chain of asking question ever stop?

Yes.

When the process reaches the person in the very front, they reach their hand forward to tap the next shoulder, but they feel only empty air.

Because there is no chair and no person in front of them, they do not ask the question. They immediately reply:

> "I am sitting in Row 1."
---

[Ponder Section : End]

[Story Continues]
So the question reaches person sitting in row 1, and he turns back and reply : "I am sitting in Row 1."

The answer now starts travelling backwards.

- Row 1 tells Row 2, **"I am in Row 1."**
- Row 2 says, **"Then I must be in Row 2,"** and tells Row 3.
- Row 3 says, **"Then I must be in Row 3,"** and tells Row 4.

This continues until the person (Tarun) sitting directly in front of Raghav says:

> **"I am in Row 11."**

Raghav immediately knows:

> **"Then I must be in Row 12."**


[Summary Section Start]
Lets ask some question to see what happened.

1. Did everyone follow the same steps?
Yes, If they knew their row number, they would tell it directly. If they did not know, they asked the person sitting infront of himself, waited for their answer, and told their row number by adding one to it.

2.  Did everyone solve the same kind of problem?
Yes, everyone solved the same problem of finding their row number.

3. What caused the process to stop?
The moment the question reaches the person sitting in row 1, it stops. Since there is no one infront of him to ask.

4.  Why did everyone have to wait before answering?
Each person has to wait (except the person sitting in the first row), because to find their row number, they needed the row number of person sitting infront of them.
