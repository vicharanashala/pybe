Three exits, three sizes

Hanuman's search could end in three different ways — moving on, stopping early, or completing the mission. In Python, each of these maps to a keyword: continue, break, and return.

return — mission over, ring handed off
break — stops searching the current mountain
continue — moves on to the next mountain

Three lines from one mountain range

return

Hanuman gave the ring to Sita and his mission was done. Mountain Three was never visited — there was nothing left to do. In Python, return ends the entire function right there.

break

The moment Sita was found in the park, Hanuman stopped searching Mountain Two. The temple was never checked. In Python, break exits the current loop immediately.

continue

Mountain One turned up empty, so Hanuman flew straight to Mountain Two. He didn't linger — he moved on. In Python, continue skips to the next lap of the loop.
