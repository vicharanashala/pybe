The Village Library
Scene 1: The Messy Bookshelf

In the sunlit courtyard of Fulera village's Aanganwadi center, Prahlad Kaka runs a weekend library.

A fresh stack of books has just been donated, but they are all mixed up on the shelf. The students want to read, but the tall books are hiding the short ones.

Prahlad Kaka tells young Raju, "The books need to be arranged neatly from shortest to tallest before we open the library."

Student
"Where's my comic? The dictionary is hiding it!"
Raju
"What a mess..."
Priya watched from the doorway. Interesting...
Too Many Books
Scene 2: The Overwhelming Task

Raju tries to look at all the books at once, picking one up, looking for the right spot, and getting totally confused.

Raju
"Too many books, Kaka! Where do I start?"

Prahlad Kaka takes a calm sip of his chai and smiles.

Prahlad Kaka
"Don't sort everything at once, Raju. Just compare two books next to each other."
Ah, breaking down the problem, Priya noted.
Comparing Neighbors
Scene 3: The Simple Rule

Prahlad Kaka explains the rule: "Start at the left end of the shelf. Compare the first book with the book right next to it."

Prahlad Kaka
"If the left book is taller, swap them. Then move one step right."

Raju finds a massive encyclopedia next to a small storybook and swaps them.

Raju
"Encyclopedia is taller! Swap, and step right."
A very logical approach.
The First Pass
Scene 4: The Heavy Books "Bubble" Up

Raju keeps comparing and swapping that heavy encyclopedia all the way down the shelf.

Because it's the tallest book, it keeps winning every comparison until it reaches the very end. The tallest book has safely "bubbled" to its final spot!

Raju
"It made it to the end! Again?"
Prahlad Kaka
"Yes, start over. But ignore the last book now."
Checking for Swaps
Scene 5: The Optimization

Raju realizes that with each pass, the next tallest book bubbles to its correct position, so he doesn't have to check the sorted books at the end anymore.

Suddenly, during one sweep across the shelf, he reaches the end of the unsorted section without making a single swap.

Raju
"Shorter... Shorter... Zero swaps!"
Prahlad Kaka
"Stop! Zero swaps means they are perfectly ordered!"
Efficiency at its finest, Priya smiled.
The Summary
Scene 6: Priya's Question

Priya steps forward from the doorway, amazed by the organized shelf.

Priya
"Wait, how did you fix that huge mess so fast?"
Raju
"It's easy! First, you compare two books next to each other."
Prahlad Kaka
"If the left one is taller, you swap them, and step right."
Raju
"When you start over, you ignore the books at the end that are sorted!"
Prahlad Kaka
"And you repeat until you sweep the whole shelf with zero swaps."
Compare, swap, ignore the end, repeat. I've got it!
Sorting Complete
Scene 7: The Perfect Library

The chaotic pile of books is now a beautifully organized shelf.

By bubbling the tallest books to the right, Raju organized the entire library. The children cheer!

Student
"I found my comic book!"
Prahlad Kaka
"Well done, Raju."
