**1\. Purpose**

The Evolution Engine governs the controlled evolution of the Code Katha Learning Intelligence System.

Its purpose is to ensure that CKLIS continuously improves through evidence, review, and structured change while preserving its educational philosophy, constitutional principles, and architectural integrity.

Evolution SHALL be deliberate rather than accidental.

---

**2\. Scope**

The Evolution Engine SHALL:

* Manage specification changes.  
* Evaluate proposed improvements.  
* Govern versioning.  
* Maintain backward compatibility where practical.  
* Preserve traceability across revisions.  
* Record the rationale behind every approved change.

The Evolution Engine SHALL NOT:

* Override the Constitution.  
* Modify historical specifications without version control.  
* Introduce undocumented changes.  
* Bypass Quality Engine approval.

---

**3\. Evolution Principles**

---

**EP-01 — Stability Before Innovation**

Changes SHALL improve the framework without destabilizing existing educational principles.

Novelty alone is insufficient justification for modification.

---

**EP-02 — Evidence-Driven Evolution**

Every significant change SHOULD be supported by one or more of the following:

* Educational research  
* Quality reports  
* User feedback  
* Controlled experimentation  
* Architectural analysis

---

**EP-03 — Traceable Decisions**

Every approved modification SHALL record:

* Why the change was proposed  
* What changed  
* Which specifications were affected  
* Who approved the change  
* Which version introduced it

---

**EP-04 — Constitutional Integrity**

No modification may contradict the Charter or Constitution.

If a proposed improvement requires constitutional change, the Constitutional Amendment Process SHALL be followed.

---

**EP-05 — Controlled Complexity**

Evolution SHOULD simplify the framework whenever possible.

Additional complexity SHALL be introduced only when it produces measurable educational or architectural benefits.

---

**4\. Evolution Triggers**

The Evolution Engine SHALL recognize the following trigger categories.

**ET-01 — Educational Research**

New evidence suggests improved instructional methods.

---

**ET-02 — Quality Findings**

Recurring failures identified by the Quality Engine.

---

**ET-03 — Implementation Experience**

Repeated implementation challenges reveal architectural weaknesses.

---

**ET-04 — Learner Outcomes**

Observed learning outcomes indicate opportunities for improvement.

---

**ET-05 — Technical Advancement**

Advances in AI, educational technology, or software engineering justify architectural refinement.

---

**ET-06 — Governance Review**

Periodic architectural reviews identify opportunities for increased consistency, clarity, or maintainability.

---

**5\. Change Classification**

Every proposed modification SHALL be classified.

**EC-01 — Editorial**

Grammar, formatting, or clarification.

No behavioral impact.

---

**EC-02 — Minor**

Small improvements that preserve existing behavior.

Backward compatible.

---

**EC-03 — Major**

Behavioral changes affecting one or more engines.

Migration guidance required.

---

**EC-04 — Constitutional**

Changes affecting constitutional principles or project philosophy.

Requires Constitutional Amendment approval.

---

**6\. Versioning Policy**

CKLIS SHALL use Semantic Versioning.

| Version Component | Meaning |
| ----- | ----- |
| Major | Breaking architectural or behavioral changes |
| Minor | New capabilities with backward compatibility |
| Patch | Editorial corrections, clarifications, or non-behavioral improvements |

Examples:

* 1.0.0 → Initial release  
* 1.1.0 → New engine capability  
* 1.1.2 → Documentation correction  
* 2.0.0 → Breaking architectural redesign

---

**7\. Change Proposal Schema**

Every proposed modification SHOULD contain:

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| Proposal ID | Unique identifier |
| Title | Short description |
| Category | EC classification |
| Motivation | Reason for change |
| Evidence | Supporting information |
| Affected Specifications | Impacted documents |
| Compatibility Assessment | Backward compatibility analysis |
| Risk Assessment | Potential negative effects |
| Recommendation | Accept, Revise, Reject |

Incomplete proposals SHALL NOT proceed to evaluation.

---

**8\. Validation Gate**

Before approval, every change SHALL pass the following validation checks.

VG-01

Maintains constitutional compliance.

VG-02

Preserves educational philosophy.

VG-03

Improves or preserves learning quality.

VG-04

Maintains architectural consistency.

VG-05

Includes complete documentation.

VG-06

Includes traceability.

Failure of any mandatory validation gate SHALL prevent approval.

# 9\. Evolution Workflow

All changes to CKLIS SHALL follow a controlled lifecycle.

Identify Improvement Opportunity  
              ↓  
Create Change Proposal  
              ↓  
Collect Supporting Evidence  
              ↓  
Impact Analysis  
              ↓  
Quality Review  
              ↓  
Governance Decision  
      ↓          ↓  
 Approved     Rejected  
      ↓  
Version Assignment  
      ↓  
Specification Update  
      ↓  
Changelog Entry  
      ↓  
Publication  
Every approved change SHALL complete this workflow before becoming part of an official CKLIS release.

---

# 10\. Decision Authority

Change approval SHALL be proportional to the impact of the proposed modification.

| Change Category | Approval Requirement |
| ----- | ----- |
| EC-01 — Editorial | Documentation review |
| EC-02 — Minor | Quality review and governance approval |
| EC-03 — Major | Full architectural review |
| EC-04 — Constitutional | Constitutional amendment process and governance approval |

Higher-impact changes SHALL require stronger evidence and more comprehensive review.

---

# 11\. Compatibility Policy

CKLIS SHALL prioritize backward compatibility whenever practical.

The following compatibility levels are defined.

## **CP-01 — Fully Compatible**

Existing implementations continue to function without modification.

---

## **CP-02 — Migration Required**

Existing implementations remain functional but SHOULD be updated using documented migration guidance.

---

## **CP-03 — Breaking Change**

Existing implementations require modification before adopting the new version.

Breaking changes SHALL be introduced only through a major version release.

---

# 12\. Migration Strategy

When compatibility cannot be preserved, the Evolution Engine SHALL require a migration plan.

A migration plan SHOULD include:

* Summary of changes

* Affected specifications

* Required implementation changes

* Deprecated behaviors

* Replacement guidance

* Expected migration effort

Migration guidance SHALL accompany every major release.

---

# 13\. Deprecation Policy

Features, processes, or specifications SHOULD be deprecated before removal whenever practical.

Each deprecated element SHALL include:

* Deprecation notice

* Reason for deprecation

* Recommended replacement

* Planned removal version

Deprecation periods SHOULD provide sufficient time for existing implementations to adapt.

---

# 14\. Evolution Repository

The Evolution Engine SHALL maintain a structured repository of historical changes.

Each record SHOULD contain:

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| Change ID | Unique identifier |
| Version | Introduced version |
| Category | EC classification |
| Summary | Description of the change |
| Motivation | Why the change was made |
| Evidence | Supporting rationale |
| Affected Documents | Updated specifications |
| Compatibility Level | CP classification |
| Approval Status | Approved or Rejected |

The repository provides a permanent history of CKLIS evolution.

---

# 15\. Feedback Integration

The Evolution Engine SHALL periodically review information produced by the Quality Engine and operational experience from other engines.

Potential inputs include:

* Recurring quality failures

* Frequently requested improvements

* Repeated implementation challenges

* Emerging educational research

* Changes in AI capabilities

* Feedback from educational practitioners

Not every observation SHALL result in a specification change.

Evidence SHALL be evaluated before any modification is proposed.

---

# 16\. Continuous Improvement Cycle

CKLIS SHALL improve through an iterative evidence-based process.

Implementation  
        ↓  
Quality Evaluation  
        ↓  
Evidence Collection  
        ↓  
Improvement Proposal  
        ↓  
Governance Review  
        ↓  
Framework Evolution  
        ↓  
Next Stable Release  
This cycle ensures that the framework evolves without sacrificing consistency or educational integrity.

---

# 17\. Governance Integration

The Evolution Engine coordinates with the broader governance architecture.

* The **Quality Engine** identifies issues and opportunities.

* The **Evolution Engine** determines whether the framework itself should change.

* The **Constitution** defines the boundaries that evolution cannot violate.

* The **Project Charter** preserves the long-term vision.

Together, these components balance innovation with stability.

---

# Final Declaration

The Evolution Engine provides the structured change-management process for the Code Katha Learning Intelligence System.

By governing versioning, compatibility, evidence-based improvements, and controlled specification updates, it ensures that CKLIS remains adaptable without compromising its educational philosophy, architectural consistency, or constitutional principles.
