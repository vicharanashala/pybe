You are rebuilding the Sorting Hat as a Python program for Hogwarts' digital enrollment system. You have a dataset of 200 past students with their personality scores (bravery: 0–10, cunning: 0–10, loyalty: 0–10, intelligence: 0–10) and the house they were sorted into.

Task 1: Given a new student's scores, determine which house to assign them. How do you compare?

Task 2: 3000 students are enrolling simultaneously. For each, you need to quickly find the most "similar" past student to use as a reference. How do you search efficiently?

Task 3: The Hat sometimes reconsiders. A student in Slytherin can appeal. How does your system handle re-classification without re-evaluating the entire dataset?
